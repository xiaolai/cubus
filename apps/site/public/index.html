How it works

Three steps, and a cube that stays in your hands.

1

Show each side to the camera.

The camera opens with the screen and a sticker detector reads each side on your device. Show the six sides in any order; each tile fills in as it is read. A misread sticker can be corrected by hand, and a cube can be painted in without a camera at all.

2

Follow the moves, one at a time.

Every solution is twenty moves or fewer. Each turn plays on the 3D cube, with the sides the camera cannot see floating beside it, and the net keeps track of where you are. Step forward, watch a move again, go back, or play it through.

3

Scramble it, and go again.

A scramble is drawn from a random state, the way competition scrambles are made, and played out turn by turn so you can set the cube up yourself. Then hand it to the camera and solve it back.

What is inside

Built to be trusted by a beginner.

Reads on your device

The sticker detector runs on your computer. No picture is kept, and none is sent anywhere.

Twenty moves or fewer

A two-phase solver written from the published method. The ceiling is a setting: twenty, nineteen, eighteen, or the shortest it can find.

Every side at once

The 3D cube floats the faces the camera angle hides beside it, so all six sides are readable without turning anything.

Real scrambles

Random-state scrambles from a cryptographic source, so a scramble is never easier than it looks.

A smart cube is optional

Bluetooth smart cubes are supported and every screen works without one. The camera always comes first.

Two shapes, three themes

A landscape or a portrait window; White, Cream or Night, following your system by default.

Download

Desktop, today.

Every build is on the latest release page. Installed apps check for a new version once a day and update themselves.

PlatformHowNotes
macOS Homebrew, or the .dmg from the release page.
brew install --cask xiaolai/tap/cubus
 One universal build for Apple silicon and Intel. Signed with a Developer ID and notarized, so it opens without a warning.
Windows The .msi or the setup .exe from the release page. Not yet code-signed: SmartScreen shows "Windows protected your PC" on install. Choose More info, then Run anyway. Each self-update asks the same way.
Linux The .deb, .rpm or .AppImage from the release page. Ubuntu 22.04 or newer, or the equivalent. Unsigned, as is usual; updates are verified before they are installed.
Phones and the browser Not yet. The iPhone and Android apps are in the source tree and build, but are not shipped. The web app is not hosted yet; this page is the introduction.

Privacy

Nothing you do in Cubus leaves your device.

The camera

Frames are read by the sticker detector on your device. No frame is stored or transmitted.

The one request

The desktop app asks GitHub once a day whether a newer version exists. That is the only network request it makes. There is no account, no analytics, no crash reporting.

The details

Every sentence of the privacy statement is a fact about the source code that a test can check.
