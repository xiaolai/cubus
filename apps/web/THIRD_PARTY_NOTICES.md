## 2. The detector model and its training data

The sticker detector (`cube-yolo.onnx`, `.mlpackage`, `.tflite` — one checkpoint, three exports) is a
YOLO model trained with [Ultralytics](https://github.com/ultralytics/ultralytics) 8.4.126,
which is **AGPL-3.0**. Ultralytics' stated position is that the licence reaches models trained with
their software and applications that use those models; that is why cubus is AGPL-3.0 rather than
permissive (`LICENSE-COMMERCIAL.md`).

The real photographs it was trained and tested on are Roboflow Universe datasets published under
**CC BY 4.0** (<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/>). Attribution, one line per dataset,
as the licence requires wherever the model ships:

- "lazycube" by lazycube, Roboflow Universe, <https://universe.roboflow.com/lazycube/lazycube>, licensed under CC BY 4.0 — training + IID test.
- "rubik-s-cube-sticker-detection" by rubiks-cube-detection, Roboflow Universe, <https://universe.roboflow.com/rubiks-cube-detection/rubik-s-cube-sticker-detection>, licensed under CC BY 4.0 — training + IID test.
- "rubyrizz" by main-d3i3y, Roboflow Universe, <https://universe.roboflow.com/main-d3i3y/rubyrizz>, licensed under CC BY 4.0 — training + IID test.
- "rubik-cube-wfbq4" by saif-kazi76-gmail-com, Roboflow Universe, <https://universe.roboflow.com/saif-kazi76-gmail-com/rubik-cube-wfbq4>, licensed under CC BY 4.0 — training + IID test.

- "rubik-s-cube-sticker-detection-rxdj9" by rubix-project, Roboflow Universe, <https://universe.roboflow.com/rubix-project/rubik-s-cube-sticker-detection-rxdj9>, licensed under CC BY 4.0 — held-out evaluation only.

## 3. Desktop and mobile binaries — Rust crates

522 third-party crates link into the cubus binary on at least one platform (macOS,
Windows, Linux, iOS, Android), resolved from `Cargo.lock` with `cargo tree --target all`; build
scripts and test-only dependencies are not in the binary and are not listed. Where a crate offers
a choice of licences ("MIT OR Apache-2.0") it is used under the one its group is headed by, and
the full expression follows each name. The Rust standard library, statically linked into every
binary, is MIT OR Apache-2.0 (<https://github.com/rust-lang/rust>). The project's own crates —
`cube-ble`, `cube-vision`, `cubus-desktop`, `optimal-solver` — are AGPL-3.0-only.

The native shells are [Tauri](https://tauri.app) 2.11.5 (MIT OR Apache-2.0),
which renders the web app in the platform's own webview (WebKit on Apple platforms, WebView2 on
Windows, WebKitGTK on Linux, Android System WebView) — a system component, not redistributed.

## 4. Platform runtimes bundled with or embedded in the binaries

- **Windows — onnxruntime and DirectML.** The native scanner links Microsoft's ONNX Runtime (MIT,
  <https://github.com/microsoft/onnxruntime/blob/main/LICENSE>) as prebuilt by pyke for the `ort` crate
  (ort-sys 2.0.0-rc.13, <https://github.com/pykeio/ort>), with the DirectML execution provider.
  DirectML itself (`DirectML.dll`) is Microsoft's DirectX Machine Learning redistributable, licensed
  under the Microsoft Software License Terms for DirectML (proprietary; redistribution inside
  applications is permitted — <https://www.nuget.org/packages/Microsoft.AI.DirectML/>). The
  provider loads it from beside the executable; the release workflow bundles it into both Windows
  installers and asserts it is there (`.github/workflows/release.yml`, the two-phase Windows
  build), while a local `tauri build` leaves it in `target/release/` only. Without it the scanner
  falls back to onnxruntime's CPU provider and says so in the log.
- **macOS and iOS — Swift.** `crates/cube-vision/swift` (the project's own, AGPL-3.0-only) is linked
  into the binary; on iOS the Swift standard libraries are embedded in the app, under the Apache

  AVFoundation and CoreBluetooth are operating-system frameworks, not redistributed.
- **Android — Kotlin and AndroidX.** The Android shell's Kotlin plugins depend on the libraries
